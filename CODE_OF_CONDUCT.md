# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and kindness;
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, threats, sexualized
language or attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing
another person's private information, and sustained disruption of project
work.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issue
reports, and other contributions that violate this policy. They may also ban a
participant temporarily or permanently when appropriate.

Report conduct concerns through a private contact method listed on a
maintainer's GitHub profile. If none is available, open a content-free issue
requesting private contact. Do not include incident details, unrelated secrets,
or personal data in that issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.
